Detailed Description

Concrete implementation of environmental sensing and audio monitoring logic.

SensorService runs two main background tasks:

  1. A sensor task that periodically reads I2C sensors (Temperature, Humidity, Lux) and the internal battery voltage.
  2. A microphone task that analyzes audio bursts to detect significant acoustic energy or patterns, dispatching events to the core when thresholds are met.
